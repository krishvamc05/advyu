Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about setting up your workspace, connecting custom domains, and pricing.

Do I need an existing marketing agency to launch ads?

No. Our AI Ads Launcher is built precisely to eliminate the need for agency overhead. You connect your ad account and configure simple parameters, and our system optimizes copy and audiences automatically.

Can I map my own custom domains to funnels?

Yes. You can link unlimited custom domains or subdomains to your landing pages, checkouts, and booking calendars. We provision SSL certificates on AWS automatically.

Is there a contract or commitment period?

No. Advyu is billed month-to-month. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription directly from your settings at any time without penalty.

How does the WhatsApp API integration work?

Our growth engine integrates with Official WhatsApp Business APIs. We assist with meta verification and API activation, enabling you to deploy automated message schedules and broadcasts without risk of blockages.
